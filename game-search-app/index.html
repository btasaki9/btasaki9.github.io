Overview

Ocarina of Time is an action-adventure game developed and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo 64. It was released in 1998 and is widely regarded as one of the greatest video games of all time. The game follows the protagonist, Link, as he embarks on a quest to stop the evil Ganondorf from obtaining the Triforce, a powerful artifact that grants its holder ultimate power.
